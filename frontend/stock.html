Reading the chart

The green or red line shows the price over the time you picked. The bars at the bottom show volume, which is how many shares were traded in that period.

A price going up does not mean it will keep going up. Prices move for many reasons and nobody can predict them perfectly.

Prices come from Yahoo Finance and can be up to about 15 minutes behind. The chart updates by itself while this page is open.
