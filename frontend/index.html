How it works

Sign up, pick a company, practice trade.

Make an account. If you are under 18 you add a parent or guardian so they know what you are up to. Then search for a company you know, like a game maker or a shoe brand, and open its chart. You start with $10,000 of virtual cash to buy and sell, and your portfolio changes as prices move.

What is in it

  • ChartsZoom, pan and hover over prices from one day to the max range. Download any chart as an image.
  • DrawingLines, arrows, boxes, circles and text that stick to the dates and prices you drew them on.
  • PortfolioYour cash, holdings, profit and loss, and every practice trade.
  • WatchlistKeep an eye on companies without buying anything.
  • NotesWrite down why you made a move, then look back later and see if you were right.

For younger users

  • Everything uses pretend money, there is no way to spend real money.
  • Users under 18 add a parent or guardian when they sign up.
  • Prices are real market data, up to about 15 minutes behind, and the site says so.
  • Nothing here is financial advice and nothing promises you will make money.
