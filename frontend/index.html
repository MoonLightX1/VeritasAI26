How it works

Three steps and you are practicing.

Sign up

Make an account. If you are under 18 you add a parent or guardian so they know what you are up to.

Pick a company

Search for a company you know, like a game maker, a shoe brand or a phone company, and open its chart.

Practice trade

Buy and sell with your $10,000 of virtual cash and see how your portfolio changes over time.
