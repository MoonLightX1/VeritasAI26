Look at real prices for 20 big companies, make trades with a $10,000 starting balance, and see a forecast based on the news. We made it so children can learn how the market works without risking anything.

What's in it

  • ChartsZoom, drag and hover. You can draw on them and save them as a picture.
  • ForecastA dashed line and shaded range built from recent prices and news headlines.
  • PortfolioYour cash, your shares, and every trade you made.
  • Watchlist and notesFollow companies, and write down why you made a move.
